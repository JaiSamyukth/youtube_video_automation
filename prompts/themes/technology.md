Theme guide: TECHNOLOGY
- How something we use every day really works (GPS, Wi-Fi, chips, AI models, the internet's undersea cables),
  the hidden history of an invention, or the one engineering trick that made something possible.
- Explain the mechanism visually: go inside the device, follow the signal, shrink the stickman.
- Accuracy matters most here; simplify without saying anything false. No product shilling, no rumours.
