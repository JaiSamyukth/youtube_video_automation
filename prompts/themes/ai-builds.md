Theme guide: AI-BUILDS (the tech behind what people build now)
- How AI agents, automation and modern software actually work, shown as one concrete trick or mechanism:
  how an agent "uses" a browser, why AI cites sources, how text becomes SQL, how spaced repetition beats cramming,
  how a restaurant order reaches the kitchen in a second, why most automations break, what an API really is.
- Audience: students, developers, founders and small-business owners who build or buy software.
- Show the mechanism visually: the stickman inside the pipeline, the data as a moving object, the agent as a
  small worker clicking real buttons.
- Accuracy first. No hype, no "AI will replace you" fear, no claims about other companies' products.
- When the topic names a product, it is ours: it is the worked example of the idea, never the point of the Short.
