Theme guide: VS MATCHUPS (absurd, fun, fact-decided)
- Format: "X vs Y" with 3 rounds decided by real facts (speed, strength, numbers, physics, history),
  a midpoint where the expected winner is in trouble, and a final round that settles it with a surprising fact.
- Great matchups: Earth vs an alien invasion, 1 gorilla vs 100 men, Roman legion vs medieval knights,
  every ant on Earth vs every human, Zeus vs Thor (as their own myths describe them), T-rex vs a tank,
  the Sun vs a black hole, Sparta vs Samurai.
- Follow the content policy VS rules exactly: no living people, no politicians as fighters, living-faith
  figures only as a respectful comparison of their teachings, never a fight or a winner.
- Stylised cartoon action only, no gore. The commentator voice is hype but every claim is true.
