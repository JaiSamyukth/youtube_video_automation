Theme guide: FITNESS, MOTIVATION AND MONEY
- One practical, evidence-based idea per Short: a training principle, a habit mechanism, a money concept
  (compound interest, lifestyle creep, opportunity cost, emergency funds), a mindset shift backed by research.
- Show it as a stickman's before/after or a race between two characters making different choices.
- No get-rich promises, no income screenshots, no named stocks/coins, no extreme diets or supplements, no
  medical advice. Motivation must be earned by a real insight, not shouted.
