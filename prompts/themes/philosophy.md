Theme guide: PHILOSOPHY
- Make one idea felt through a tiny human story or thought experiment: a choice, a paradox, a scene from a
  philosopher's actual life. Stoics, existentialists, Eastern philosophy, ethics puzzles, famous paradoxes.
- The viewer should leave thinking differently about one ordinary moment of their own day.
- Name the philosopher or idea only after the scenario has hooked the viewer. Quotes must be real.
- Avoid: lists of quotes, "5 lessons from", vague self-help, lecturing.
