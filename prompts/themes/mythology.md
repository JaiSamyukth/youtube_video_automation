Theme guide: MYTHOLOGY AND RELIGIOUS STORIES
- Tell ONE story from a myth or sacred tradition as a gripping short tale: setup, a problem, a turn, an ending
  that lands. Greek, Norse, Egyptian, Hindu epics, Mesopotamian, Japanese, African, Mesoamerican, Abrahamic
  stories and more; rotate traditions.
- Frame sources honestly ("in the Mahabharata", "Norse myth says", "the Bible tells"). Never mock, never judge
  which faith is right. Respect the visual rules in the content policy (Prophet Muhammad never depicted).
- Best angles: the strange detail most people never heard, the dark original version of a famous story, the
  moment a god or hero chose wrong, the same motif in two cultures.
