# Content policy (protects the channels from strikes, demonetisation and defamation)

Facts
- Every factual claim must be true and widely supported. Never invent studies, quotes, names, dates or numbers.
- Myths, scripture and legends are told as stories: "the myth says", "in the Mahabharata", "followers believe".
  Never present a religious claim as historical fact or as false.
- Quotes attributed to real people must be real and correctly attributed; otherwise paraphrase without quote marks.

Religion and mythology
- Tell stories with respect. No mockery, satire or ranking of any living faith, deity, prophet or sacred figure.
- Never depict the Prophet Muhammad (or other prophets of Islam) in any visual form, not even as a stick figure,
  silhouette or symbol standing in for him. Islamic stories may be told with landscapes, objects and text only.
- Gods of living traditions (Hindu, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jewish and others) may appear only respectfully,
  never as fighters in a brawl, punchlines or caricatures. Ancient pantheons no longer worshipped at scale
  (Greek, Norse, Egyptian, Mesopotamian, Aztec) may appear as characters and may clash, as in their own myths.

VS matchups
- Allowed: hypothetical and fictional matchups (Earth vs aliens, 1 gorilla vs 100 men, Roman legion vs modern
  SWAT, T-rex vs tank), animals, armies, machines, natural forces, ancient myths' own gods and monsters,
  fictional archetypes.
- Living-faith figures: only as a respectful "what each tradition actually teaches" comparison
  (e.g. "Jesus and Krishna: two teachers, one question"), never as a fight, a winner or a loser.
- Real people: NO living people at all, no politicians of any era as fighters, and no comparing anyone to
  Hitler or other perpetrators of atrocities. Historical figures may only be compared on documented facts
  (e.g. "Alexander vs Genghis Khan: whose empire really ran further?"), never as glorification of violence.
- Every round in a matchup is decided by real facts (speed, strength, numbers, history), stated in the narration.

Health, money, safety
- No medical, legal or personalised financial advice. No "do this and get rich" promises, no income claims,
  no named stocks or coins to buy. Teach principles and well-supported research findings.
- Fitness: no extreme diets, no dangerous challenges, no steroid or supplement endorsements.
- No instructions for weapons, self-harm, or anything dangerous to copy.

Platform safety
- No graphic violence or gore (battles are stylised, no blood close-ups), no sexual content, no hate.
- No clickbait lies: the title and hook must be delivered by the video.
- Not made for kids. Original script and original visuals every time (YouTube "inauthentic content" rule).
